About Us Veltrix: affordable local car rental in Malaysia

Veltrix is a car rental management system built for one clear purpose: renting out Malaysian national cars only (Proton and Perodua) to customers in Malaysia, at a low and fair price for everyone.

  • Local cars onlyOur whole fleet is Proton and Perodua, such as Myvi, Axia, Bezza, Saga, Persona, Ativa, Alza, X50 and X70. We do not rent imported or luxury brands.
  • Low price, same for everyoneEvery customer pays the same daily rate for the same car. There are no VIP or Regular tiers and no hidden membership pricing.
  • Why local brands?Proton and Perodua cars are the most familiar cars on Malaysian roads. Their running costs, spare parts and servicing are easy to find and affordable, so we can keep rental rates low.
  • Who it is forStudents, families, workers and tourists who need a simple, budget-friendly car for a day, a weekend or a balik kampung trip.
